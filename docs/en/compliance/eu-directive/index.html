EU Whistleblowing Directive

Directive (EU) 2019/1937 sets the rules for internal reporting channels across the EU. Each member state turns it into its own law; in Germany that is the HinSchG. This page is a summary, not legal advice.

Requirement by requirement

The binding text is on EUR-Lex.
