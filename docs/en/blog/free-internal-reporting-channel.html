A free internal reporting channel: what "free" really costs

Getting a whistleblowing tool for free is easy. The duties under the Whistleblower Protection Act (HinSchG) remain all the same. What is left in server, operations and work, which options exist and how to start with OpenWhistle.

27 September 2026 6 min read OpenWhistle Contributors

A free whistleblowing system saves the licence. It does not save the internal reporting office (interne Meldestelle). Employers with, as a rule, at least 50 employees must set one up and run it (§ 12 HinSchG). The law does not ask what the software cost.

Transparency notice

This article is written by the developers of OpenWhistle. Other providers' prices are shown as the providers publish them, retrieved on 27 September 2026. Not legal advice.

The software is the smallest item. Even with a paid service, the people, the telephone and the follow-up stay with you.

How to start with OpenWhistle

  1. Try the live demo, as a whistleblower and as the reporting office.
  2. Provision a Linux server with Docker 24 or newer.
  3. Follow the installation guide: clone, fill in .env, docker compose up -d.
  4. In the wizard at /setup, create the first account with mandatory TOTP.
  5. Set up categories, locations and accounts, then send a test report.

The step-by-step guide shows every step with commands. The HinSchG compliance guide sums up which duties remain afterwards.
