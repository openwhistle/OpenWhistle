Roles

RoleMay
Superadmineverything an admin may, plus organisations (/admin/organisations). Sees every organisation, whatever its own org_id. The setup wizard creates one.
Admineverything, including users, categories, locations, the audit log and report deletion
Case Managerview, comment on and advance the reports assigned to them. The sidebar shows dashboard, statistics and telephone channel; no users, no deletion

Accounts are managed at /admin/users. A new account is a case manager unless another role is chosen; an unknown role is refused. The last active admin cannot be deactivated, so nobody is locked out. Only a superadmin creates, changes, deactivates or reactivates a superadmin. In DEMO_MODE the demo accounts cannot be changed. Only admins may dismiss the IP-header warning.
