Privacy policy

This is a translation. The German version is binding. Last updated: 9 October 2026.

Who is responsible

E-mail: info@openwhistle.net.

In short

  • This website sets no cookies and has no analytics tool and no tracking. We count page views only from the server log, without IP address or browser identification.
  • It loads nothing from other servers; the fonts are here too.
  • It is served from our own server. The server does not store your IP address.

Delivery from our own server

This website is served from the controller's own server. The server is hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen. Hetzner processes the data on our behalf under Art. 28 GDPR.

A reverse proxy in front of it writes no access log and no error log. For each request, the website container logs only the date and time, the path served, the status code and the host name of the referring site. It stores no IP address, no browser identification and no query string.

These lines stay 30 days on the server and 90 days in our log archive. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is operating and securing the website and counting page views. To deliver the page, the server processes your IP address for the duration of the connection; it is not stored.

No cookies, no analytics tool, no tracking, no third-party content

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics tool and does not track you. We count page views only from the server log described above, without IP address or browser identification. Fonts, images and scripts come from this website itself. Your browser asks no other server for them.

A stored setting: light or dark

If you choose a colour scheme, your browser stores that choice under the name ow-theme in its local storage. It does not leave your device. The legal basis is § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG: storing it is strictly necessary for the service you asked for. You delete it with your browser's site data.

Searching the documentation

The search runs entirely in your browser. For this, it loads a search index from this website. Your search term itself is never sent, but from the parts of the index your browser loads, the server could roughly infer what you searched for.

E-mail to us

If you write to info@openwhistle.net, we process your address, the content and the time in order to reply. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We delete the e-mail once the request is settled and no statutory retention duty applies.

The e-mail is kept on the controller's own mail server. The server is hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen. Hetzner processes the data on our behalf under Art. 28 GDPR.

The mail server logs every delivery: sender, recipient, time and the delivering server. These logs stay 30 days on the server and 365 days in our log archive. They serve to prove abuse and attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Post to our address

To manage the post sent to us, we use the services of IMPRESSUM- PRIVATSCHUTZ GmbH, Ludwig-Erhard-Str. 18, 20459 Hamburg. This service provider offers a secure and reliable postal address for our project (e.g. the legal notice, the privacy policy, the cancellation policy and possibly other areas). This is also our legitimate interest within the meaning of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We have concluded a data processing agreement with Impressum-Privatschutz and fully implement the strict requirements of the German data protection authorities when using this service. You can find more information about data protection at Impressum-Privatschutz GmbH here: https://impressum-privatschutz.de/datenschutzerklaerung/

Support through GitHub Sponsors

This website links to GitHub Sponsors. If you follow the link, you leave this website. GitHub processes any payment, not this website. GitHub's privacy statement applies.

The demo at demo.openwhistle.net

The same controller runs the demo, on his server at Hetzner Online GmbH. Hetzner processes the demo's data on our behalf under Art. 28 GDPR. Its web server writes no access log and no error log, and the application stores no IP address.

Everything you enter in the demo is visible to every visitor. It is deleted every 6 hours. Do not enter real reports there.

The demo sets only cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work. ow_csrf protects every form against forged requests and lasts until the browser session ends. ow-submission-session and ow-status-session hold a report and its status check together, each for at most 2 hours. ow_session keeps you signed in to the admin area until the login expires. ow-lang remembers the language you chose for one year. Your browser stores your colour scheme under ow-theme, as on this website. The legal basis is § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG: storing them is strictly necessary for the service you asked for.

The application's operational messages contain no IP addresses. They stay 30 days on the server and 90 days in our log archive, so that we can find faults (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You may object to processing under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). To do so, write to info@openwhistle.net.

You may complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of your habitual residence or place of work (Art. 77 GDPR).
