Whistleblowing software providers compared 2026: open source, managed and SaaS

How to compare providers of whistleblowing systems, and what five of them publish. Only verifiable facts, each with its source and retrieval date.

27 April 2026 Updated 27 September 2026 7 min read OpenWhistle Contributors

Transparency note

This article is written by the developers of OpenWhistle. It is therefore not neutral. We only cite facts the providers publish themselves, retrieved on 27 September 2026. We make no quality judgements about other products. Prices can change; the provider's linked page is authoritative.

Whether you buy software for the Whistleblower Protection Act (HinSchG) or run it yourself: seven criteria separate the providers. They are questions, not grades. The third column answers them for OpenWhistle.

How to compare providers

Since 2011, six independent reviewers have audited GlobaLeaks, among them Cure53 and Radically Open Security; the reports are published. The translations come from the Localization Lab community. The Digital Public Goods Alliance lists GlobaLeaks as a “Digital Public Good”. OpenWhistle has no external audit yet.

Run it yourself or have it run for you

Open source does not mean you have to run servers yourself. For GlobaLeaks there are managed offerings, such as Hinweisgeberportal OpenSource by splice digital GmbH. We know of no such offering for OpenWhistle.

What no system does for you

  • The oral channel. Reports must also be possible by telephone (§ 16 Abs. 3 HinSchG).
  • The people. Whoever handles reports must be independent and competent (§ 15).
  • The follow-up. Examining, investigating and remedying stays with the employer (§ 14 Abs. 1, § 18).
  • The contract. For every external service you need a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR.

Conclusion

GlobaLeaks is the mature open-source reference, with over 70 languages. OpenWhistle is younger and explicitly tracks the deadlines of § 17 HinSchG. Neither costs a licence fee. The managed and commercial offerings cost money and take operation off your hands in return.

Try both open-source systems before you decide: the OpenWhistle demo and the GlobaLeaks demo.
